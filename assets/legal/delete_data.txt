Delete Your Data — Kareton

Kareton (Kareton: Expenses & Mileage) stores your information locally on your device. We do not operate a Kareton cloud account.

How to delete your data

1. Inside the app
   - Delete individual expenses, trips, and documents.
   - Remove vehicles from your fleet if you no longer need them.
   - Clear Digital Pocket photos/documents you added.

2. Delete everything at once
   - Android Settings → Apps → Kareton → Storage → Clear data (or Clear storage).
   - Or uninstall Kareton. This removes all local app data on that device.

3. Backups you created
   - If you exported a backup file (.db or shared copy), delete that file yourself from your device, email, or cloud drive. Kareton does not host your backups.

What is deleted
   - Profile name, vehicles, expenses, trips, receipts, digital pocket files, budgets, reminders, and app settings on that device.

What we keep
   - Kareton does not store your data on our servers. Nothing is retained by Kareton after you clear app data or uninstall.
   - Google Play may retain purchase history according to Google’s policies (manage in play.google.com).
